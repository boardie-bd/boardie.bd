Harmonies

Build Dream-Lands, Place Landscapes & Invite Nature • 1-4 Players • Approx. 30-45 min Playtime

Game Overview

৳3920

Harmonies invites you to build a world of balance and beauty. Through clever tile placement, create lush forests, flowing rivers, and tranquil mountains while attracting animals that thrive in harmony with your landscapes. It’s a peaceful yet deeply strategic experience that rewards creativity, foresight, and your love of nature.

Why You’ll Love This Game

  • Serene and immersive – every turn feels calm and satisfying, as your landscape grows before your eyes.
  • Beautiful components – tactile wooden tiles, charming artwork, and soothing color palettes bring nature to life.
  • Accessible yet thoughtful – quick to learn, endlessly replayable, and perfect for all skill levels.
  • Solo or group play – equally rewarding alone or shared with friends and family.
  • Eco-themed gameplay – design spaces that balance wildlife and landscape — a meaningful puzzle with a positive message.

What’s in the Box

  • Beautiful main board & 4 player boards
  • 120+ wooden landscape tiles (forest, mountain, water, field)
  • Animal & Spirit cards with unique abilities
  • Scorepad, reference cards & illustrated rulebook

How to Play (in short)

• Draft and place landscape tiles to expand your natural ecosystem.
• Complete patterns to attract animals and score harmony points.
• Balance your biome — forests need rivers, animals need homes, and harmony means synergy.
• When the landscape is full, calculate your score — the most harmonious world wins!
