Virus!

Protect Your Organs • Infect Opponents • Outrageous Fun for Everyone

Game Overview

৳820

Virus! is a fast-paced, chaotic, and hilarious card game where your goal is simple: build a healthy body while sabotaging everyone else’s! Infect organs, steal cards, launch surprise outbreaks, and survive long enough to complete a full set of healthy body parts.

Why You’ll Love This Game

  • Fun for all ages – easy to learn, impossible not to laugh.
  • Wild take-that mechanics – infect your friends & dodge their attacks.
  • Compact & portable – perfect for school, home, or hangouts.
  • Replayable chaos – every round is unpredictable.

What’s in the Box

  • 70 colorful cards (Organs, Viruses, Cures, Treatments)
  • Bright and expressive artwork
  • Rulebook

How to Play (in short)

• Collect 4 healthy organs of different colors.
• Use viruses to infect opponents and stop their progress.
• Cure and protect your own organs.
• First player with a full healthy body wins!

Game Details

Players: 2–6 | Age: 8+ | Play Time: 20–30 minutes | Complexity: Light & Funny

Perfect For

  • Families looking for a silly, fast game
  • Friends who enjoy chaotic party-style gameplay
  • Casual gamers and beginners
  • Anyone who likes “take-that” humor

Get Ready to Spread the Fun

Bring home the infectious excitement of Virus! It’s easy to pick up, wildly entertaining, and perfect for game nights where laughter matters more than strategy.
