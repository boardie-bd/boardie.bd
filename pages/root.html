Game Overview

Root is a thrilling asymmetric strategy game where each faction offers a totally unique playstyle. Wage battles, form alliances, craft items, and struggle for control of the Woodland.

Why You'll Love Root

  • Deep asymmetry — every faction feels like its own game.
  • Beautiful woodland aesthetic — a hand-drawn storybook world.
  • Tactical battles & negotiation — allies today, enemies tomorrow.
  • High replay value — no two games feel the same.
  • Great for competitive players — outthink & outmaneuver.

What’s in the Box

  • Woodland map board
  • 4 fully asymmetric factions
  • Wooden meeples & tokens
  • Faction boards, cards & rulebooks

Quick How-to-Play

• Pick one of the 4 factions, each with its own rules.
• Battle, craft, explore, and expand influence.
• Use diplomacy, clever planning, or brute force.
• First player to 30 victory points rules the forest.

Perfect For

  • Strategy enthusiasts
  • Groups who enjoy competitive depth
  • Fans of negotiation & clever tactics
  • Players seeking high replayability
